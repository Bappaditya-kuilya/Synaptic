# Synaptic User Guide

## What Synaptic Is

Synaptic is a thinking workspace that turns raw ideas into a living map.

You can paste a note, a rough thought, a paragraph from research, or a product idea. Synaptic then pulls out the core concepts, connects them into a graph, and helps you keep building that knowledge over time instead of losing it in scattered notes.

It is meant to feel like a place where ideas grow, not a one-shot AI prompt box.

## Why Synaptic Feels Different

Most AI tools give you an answer and move on.

Synaptic is different because it treats your thinking as something worth keeping:
- your ideas stay connected instead of disappearing after one response
- each new input strengthens a larger workspace
- the graph helps you see structure, not just text
- you can revisit old workspaces and continue from where you stopped
- it is built for exploration, not just output

In short: other tools answer. Synaptic accumulates understanding.

## What You Can Do With It

- capture an idea and turn it into a concept map
- connect product thinking, research, strategy, and systems ideas
- build separate workspaces for different topics
- switch between workspaces as your thinking evolves
- import a saved workspace and continue building it
- export your work when you want to save or share it

## How To Use Synaptic

### 1. Open a workspace

When Synaptic loads, it opens your current workspace automatically.

You can also:
- create a new workspace
- load a starter workspace
- switch to an existing workspace

### 2. Add an idea

Write or paste:
- a note
- a paragraph
- a research summary
- a product concept
- a messy thought you want to clarify

Then choose `Expand Graph`.

### 3. Let Synaptic structure it

Synaptic extracts the main concepts, builds relationships between them, and adds them into your existing workspace.

Instead of replacing what you already had, it tries to grow the same map over time.

### 4. Explore the graph

You can:
- click nodes to inspect them
- search for concepts
- filter by category
- focus on a selected cluster

This helps you move from “what did I write?” to “how do these ideas relate?”

### 5. Save, switch, and continue later

Your workspaces are persistent.

That means you can return to:
- a product workspace
- a research workspace
- a personal idea map
- a strategy workspace

without starting over every time.

## Best Ways To Use It

Synaptic works best when you use it for:
- product planning
- research synthesis
- startup thinking
- concept exploration
- learning complex topics
- connecting ideas across multiple sessions

It is especially useful when your thinking is too interconnected for flat notes.

## Tips For Better Results

- Give it full thoughts, not only keywords.
- One clear paragraph is better than a vague phrase.
- Use separate workspaces for separate domains.
- Revisit and expand the same workspace instead of always starting fresh.
- Click around the graph after each input to understand what changed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Synaptic a note-taking app?

Not in the traditional sense.

It is closer to a thinking workspace. Notes are inputs, but the main value is the structure that grows around them.

### Is it only for AI or technical topics?

No.

You can use it for product ideas, philosophy, learning, strategy, writing, research, or anything else that benefits from connected thinking.

### Do I need to understand graphs to use it?

No.

You can treat the graph as a visual way of seeing how your ideas connect. You do not need any technical background.

### Can I keep multiple workspaces?

Yes.

You can create and switch between separate workspaces for different themes or projects.

### What if I want to start over?

You can reset the current workspace or create a completely new one.

### Can I save my work outside Synaptic?

Yes.

You can export a workspace and import it again later.

### Why not just use ChatGPT or a normal notes app?

Because those tools are optimized for answers or documents.

Synaptic is optimized for connected thought over time.

### Is Synaptic finished?

No.

It already works as a serious idea workspace, but it is still growing. Future versions will make persistence, organization, and intelligence even stronger.

## The Simple Summary

If you want a tool that helps you think in connected systems instead of isolated pages, Synaptic is built for that.
